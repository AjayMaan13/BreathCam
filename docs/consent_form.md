# Participant Information and Consent Form (DRAFT)

**Project:** BreathCam, a camera-based breathing-rate study
**Researcher:** [Your name], [email]
**Status:** Personal skills-development project. It has not been reviewed by a research ethics board and is not affiliated with any institution.

## 1. Purpose

I am building software that estimates breathing rate (breaths per minute) from video of a person sitting still, and testing how accurate it is. I need short recordings of volunteers breathing at different paces under different conditions.

## 2. What you will be asked to do

- Sit about 1-2 metres from a camera on a tripod for 60-90 second recordings, roughly [N] recordings in total, about [N] minutes overall.
- Breathe along with a metronome at different rates, and in some recordings change lighting, clothing, or distance.
- Wear a phone taped to your chest or abdomen that records motion (accelerometer) as a reference measurement.
- Optionally (tick below), breathe naturally after a brief period of light exercise.

You may decline any task and still take part in the rest.

## 3. What is recorded

- **Video of you** (face and upper body). Video of a face is identifiable.
- Phone motion data from the chest sensor.
- Conditions of each recording (pace, lighting, clothing, distance).
- Your name and signature on this form. **Your name is stored separately** from the recordings, linked only by a code (for example "S01").

No health information is collected. I do not ask about medical conditions, and this study cannot diagnose anything.

## 4. Who sees your data

- Only me, by default.
- [CHOOSE ONE and delete the other before use]
  - **Option A:** Raw video stays on my personal computer. Only face-blurred or chest-cropped versions, which cannot identify you, are ever uploaded to cloud notebook services (Google Colab / Kaggle) for model training.
  - **Option B:** Raw video stays on my personal computer and is never uploaded anywhere.
- **Published results** (a public code repository, a report, possibly a resume or interview) will contain only summary numbers, graphs of breathing signals, and, **only if you agree below**, face-blurred example frames. Raw video will not be published.

## 5. Storage and retention

- Recordings are stored on my password-protected, disk-encrypted computer: [confirm FileVault is on].
- The code-to-name key and signed forms are stored separately from the recordings and never in the code repository.
- I will delete all raw video and the code-to-name key on or before **[date, e.g. 6 months from recording]**. De-identified breathing signals and summary results may be kept.

## 6. Risks and benefits

- Risks are minimal: mild discomfort from sitting still, the sensor on your chest, and light exercise (if chosen). The privacy risk of video is addressed in sections 4 and 5.
- There is no direct benefit to you. There is no payment.

## 7. Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time during a recording session. **You can withdraw later, without giving a reason, by contacting me at [email] up to [date]; I will delete your recordings and any data derived from them.** After published results are produced, I cannot remove your contribution from aggregate numbers already published, but I will remove all of your recordings.
